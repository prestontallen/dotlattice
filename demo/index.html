dotlattice

pixel-style dot-lattice time series · one SVG · zero dependencies · hover a column

1 · grouping

One series per group, stacked outward from the baseline in group order. Same data below, all on one side vs. split up/down.

2 · density & quantum

Two weeks of minute-level events. When a column would exceed maxRows rows, one dot stands for N events — hover a tall column.

3 · overlays

Ticks with collision-stacked labels, a run of dots on the baseline, a highlight window, and top axis labels — same as servitor's dossier timeline.

4 · brush zoom + rollup

Drag across the chart to zoom into a range — the axis and rollup regenerate for the view. Rollup picks the tick unit automatically or pins it; click the chart (or hit Esc) to reset. Hover a column for its tooltip.
